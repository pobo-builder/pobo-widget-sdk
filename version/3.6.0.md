# 3.6.0

## New: scroll video
- `pb-scroll-video` plugin styles (`__poster`, `__canvas`) in both generic and legacy builds; the frames are drawn by the pobo-plugin `ScrollVideo` widget
- 14 widgets: `rc-scroll-video-full`, `-text-right`, `-text-left`, `-intro`, `-cta`, `-benefits`, `-pair`, `-portrait`, `-overlay`, `-parameter`, `-steps`, `-product`, `-grid`, `-hero`
- `__video` is the `pb-scroll-video` wrapper itself (no `__media` box around it); text areas are single rich-text blocks (heading and paragraph inside `__text`, `__head`, `__caption`, `__item`, `__header`), a `__button > __link` sits next to `__text` in `-cta` and `-hero`; headings are styled by typography, the widget variables cover layout only (`--pobo-scroll-video-<name>-video-*` on the video, `--pobo-scroll-video-<name>-box-*` on the text box, mobile overrides in `mobile-variable`)
- Files live in `src/generic/scroll-video/` (legacy copies in `src/rc-scroll-*.scss`)

## New: scroll track (pinned scrollytelling)
- `pb-scroll-track` plugin styles (`plugin/scroll-track`): the pobo-plugin picks one of two modes per section and adds the class; `pb-scroll-reveal` is optional inside a track; `prefers-reduced-motion` shows everything
- 3 story widgets: `rc-scroll-story-side` (video + `__step > __number + __text` chapters), `rc-scroll-story-overlay` (chapters `__step > __text` over the video, bottom-left), `rc-scroll-story-hero` (chapters over the video, centred `__text`, last step with a `__button > __link` using `--pobo-btn-*`)
- `pb-scroll-track--static` is the default inside a product-description column: the section flows like any other widget, the video spans the column width and plays on scroll, the layout is the pinned one at the widget's own size: `-overlay`/`-hero` chapters over the video (gradient, white text), `-side` video 58 % + chapters beside it, the video keeps its natural aspect ratio and the chapters take turns with the video progress (`is-active`/`is-passed` set by the plugin in both modes). Without the plugin (B2B, white-label, catalogue preview) or under `prefers-reduced-motion` all chapters stay visible stacked
- `pb-scroll-track--pinned` is used only when the section spans at least 85 % of the viewport or is at least 1200 px wide (the shop stretched it to the page container, or mobile) and no ancestor clips overflow. The stage is the viewport: `__pin` is `position: sticky; top: var(--pobo-scroll-track-top)` and `calc(100vh - var(--pobo-scroll-track-top))` tall, `overflow: hidden`, a centred flex box; the track is `calc(100vh + --pobo-scroll-step-count * --pobo-scroll-track-step-length)` tall (`100vh` per chapter by default); `__step` chapters are stacked in one grid cell and switched via `is-active` / `is-passed`. The track itself is `position: relative; width: 100%` (no viewport bleed). The pobo-plugin writes `--pobo-scroll-step-count` and `--pobo-scroll-track-top` (sticky-header offset, unless the shop set it) onto the track; `--pobo-scroll-pin-height` is gone
- Pinned layouts: `-side` keeps a content box (`--pobo-scroll-story-side-content-max-width` 1200px, `--pobo-scroll-story-side-content-padding` 40px, mobile 20px) with the video `--pobo-scroll-story-side-video-width` (58 %) on the left (poster `object-fit: contain`, capped at the stage height minus padding) and the chapters vertically centred on the right (`--pobo-scroll-story-side-pinned-steps-padding`); under `md` the video sits on top (`max-height: 50vh`) with the chapters below. `-overlay` and `-hero` fill the stage with the video (`object-fit: cover`, gradient `--pobo-scroll-story-<name>-video-overlay-bg`) and lay the chapters over it inside a content box (`--pobo-scroll-story-<name>-content-max-width` 1200px, `--pobo-scroll-story-<name>-content-padding` 40px, mobile 20px): overlay bottom-left with `--pobo-scroll-story-overlay-steps-max-width` (560px, mobile 100 %), hero centred with the first chapter in the middle and the rest at the bottom; the white chapter colour comes from `--pobo-scroll-story-<name>-text-color` on `__text`
- `--pobo-scroll-track-*`, `--pobo-scroll-reveal-*` and `--pobo-scroll-story-*` variables with mobile overrides; removed `--pobo-scroll-story-side-pin-padding`, `-steps-min-height`, `-step-margin` and `--pobo-scroll-story-<name>-video-max-height`; `--pobo-scroll-track-top` is no longer declared in `native-variable` (the plugin provides it)
